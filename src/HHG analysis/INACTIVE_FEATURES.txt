IA-001  Integrated ROI / pixel / harmonic Fourier aggregation
        The absent ShowScanROIFFTGUI, ShowScanPxFFTGUI and ShowScanHHFFTGUI
        do not establish whether to Fourier-transform the summed signal or
        average the separate pixel magnitudes. These differ with phase.
        User answered: "I don't know; leave the ambiguous Fourier
        aggregation inactive."
        Recreated individual-pixel Fourier views ARE active; they do not
        compute or present an ambiguous integrated Fourier curve.
        Restore after choosing aggregation, FFT normalization, DC treatment,
        and frequency-axis convention, or obtaining the original helpers.

IA-002  Original TC normalized / spatially averaged Fourier heatmap
        TConlyAnalysisFromGUIdata.m calls missing fftPerCell, averages its
        magnitude over space, then divides by the DC bin. The missing helper
        leaves its transform length, positive-half selection and frequency
        convention uncertain (the caller uses N-1 for its frequency grid).
        This is covered by IA-001; no guessed replacement is shown.
        Devon colors ARE bundled and available without crameri; its absence
        is no longer a color-palette blocker.

DL-001  Automatic grating-calibration presets
        Require MATLAB Signal Processing Toolbox findpeaks and Curve Fitting
        Toolbox fit/fittype/fitoptions. The R2026b installation inspected on
        2026-11-04 did not provide these functions. Controls report this.
        Presets: smoothed maxima (rows 40:70), old unsmoothed maxima
        (60:80), FWHM centers + direct fit (40:100).
        Restore with those licensed toolboxes; do not substitute an untested
        optimizer or use an unrelated calibration silently.
        Importing a saved, verified xW vector works without those toolboxes.
